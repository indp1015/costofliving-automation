The Hidden Cost of Housing in Coastal Cities

Published on 2026-10-20 by Data Analysis Team

It is a common misconception that everything is more expensive in major coastal cities. While overall costs are indeed high, our breakdown of the BEA Regional Price Parities reveals a surprising truth: Goods are often only slightly above the national average.

The real culprit is housing. On our county detail pages, the radar chart clearly shows that the 'Housing (Rents)' axis spikes dramatically compared to 'Goods' and 'Services'. This means that while your grocery bill might be normal, your rent is driving your overall cost of living through the roof.
