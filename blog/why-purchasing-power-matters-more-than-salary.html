Why Purchasing Power Matters More Than Your Salary

Published on 2026-10-15 by Data Analysis Team

When evaluating a job offer or considering a move, the first number most people look at is the salary. A $100,000 offer in San Francisco feels very different from a $100,000 offer in rural Ohio. This is where the concept of Purchasing Power comes in.

By dividing median income by the local Regional Price Parity, we can see the true value of a dollar in any given county. Our data shows that some of the wealthiest counties by nominal income actually rank much lower when adjusted for local housing and service costs.

Always look beyond the base salary. Use our dashboard to check the Purchasing Power Index before you sign the contract.
