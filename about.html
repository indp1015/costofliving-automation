About Cost of Living Data

This platform provides accurate, county-level cost of living data across the United States. Our goal is to help individuals, families, and researchers make informed decisions about where to live based on real economic data.

We aggregate official data from the Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) Regional Price Parities and the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS). By combining these datasets, we calculate unique metrics such as the Purchasing Power Index.

Data Sources

  • BEA Regional Price Parities (RPP): Measures price levels for goods, services, and housing.
  • Census ACS 5-Year Estimates: Provides median household income, rent, and home values.
