📊 Local Market Analysis

Owsley County presents a cost-of-living profile defined by deep structural discounts relative to the national baseline, registering an overall Regional Price Parity of 89.9 in 2023. The most striking deviation occurs in the services category, which sits at 62.9 — nearly 37 percentage points below the national average — reflecting substantially lower local pricing for healthcare, utilities, and dining. Goods, encompassing groceries, apparel, and fuel, represent the highest relative cost component at 94.5, narrowing the gap with national prices to just 5.5 percentage points. Rental costs occupy the middle ground at 80.9, translating to a median monthly rent of $551 against a national median of $928. This composition indicates that while shelter and services are markedly inexpensive, the daily basket of tangible goods exerts the strongest upward pressure on the local cost structure, preventing the overall index from falling further. Purchasing power metrics reveal a complex reality for the median household earning $31,064 annually. Although the Purchasing Power Index of 34,557.8 suggests the median income stretches further locally than its nominal value implies, the absolute income level remains less than half the national median of $65,047. Rent burden stands at a manageable 21.3 percent of gross income, a direct function of suppressed rental markets rather than high earnings. However, the low Services index (62.9) likely signals limited local availability of specialized healthcare and professional services, forcing residents to incur travel costs or accept reduced access — hidden expenses not captured in the RPP. The Goods index near parity (94.5) means food and fuel budgets consume a disproportionate share of nominal wages compared to higher-income regions, constraining discretionary spending despite low headline inflation. Demographic and labor market conditions reinforce the low-cost, low-income equilibrium. An unemployment rate of 11.3 percent — significantly above national norms — combined with an educational attainment rate of just 6.8 percent for bachelor’s degrees or higher, defines a labor market anchored in low-wage sectors with limited upward mobility. The median age of 47.9 years suggests an aging population potentially reliant on fixed incomes or shrinking labor force participation, which suppresses wage growth and sustains the demand for cheap housing and services. This demographic profile creates a feedback loop: low wages keep service prices down, while the lack of high-skill employment prevents income convergence with national averages. Over the 2010–2023 period, the RPP remained remarkably stable, declining only 0.6 percent from 90.4 to 89.9, indicating persistent price stability in the local consumption basket. Nominal median income grew 48 percent, rising from $20,985 to $31,064, yet median rent accelerated faster at 57 percent, climbing from $351 to $551. This divergence means housing costs have claimed a growing slice of household budgets despite the overall low rent burden percentage. For a prospective resident, the budgeting reality centers on a trade-off: shelter and services are deeply discounted, but goods prices track near national levels while income ceilings remain rigid. A household budget must allocate a higher-than-expected share to groceries and transportation fuel relative to income, and factor in the likelihood of traveling outside the county for specialized medical care or retail variety, effectively exporting a portion of local purchasing power to neighboring economies.

What this means for residents of Owsley County, Kentucky

With a Purchasing Power Index of 34557.8, a median household income of $31,064 in Owsley County, Kentucky has the equivalent buying power of $10,735,035 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 80.9 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
