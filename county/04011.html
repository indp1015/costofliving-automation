📊 Local Market Analysis

Greenlee County presents a cost-of-living profile that sits marginally above the national baseline, with the Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parity (RPP) for 2023 registering 100.7 against a national index of 100. This slight premium masks significant internal divergence: services command a substantial 108.6 index, driven by healthcare, utilities, and dining expenses that exceed the national average by nearly 9 percent, while goods such as groceries, apparel, and fuel come in cheaper at 97.9. The dominant factor suppressing the overall index is rental housing, which carries an exceptionally low 83.3 RPP, reflecting a median monthly rent of just $570—roughly 39 percent below the national median of $928. Consequently, daily life leans affordable for those covering shelter costs locally, though the service-heavy component ensures that discretionary spending on healthcare, childcare, or restaurant meals erodes savings faster than in lower-service-cost regions. The consumption breakdown reveals that services act as the primary inflationary driver in the local budget, offsetting the deep discount found in housing. With median household income at $75,239—surpassing the national median of $65,047—residents allocate a mere 9.1 percent of gross income to median rent, a rent-burden ratio that is strikingly low compared to national standards often exceeding 30 percent. This dynamic translates into a Purchasing Power Index of 74,684.8, indicating that after adjusting for local price levels, the median household retains real purchasing power significantly above the nominal national median income. Effectively, the housing discount functions as a substantial implicit subsidy, freeing capital for the elevated service costs or discretionary investment, provided the household participates in the local labor market. Local demographics and labor conditions reinforce this affordability narrative. An unemployment rate of 3.5 percent signals a tight labor market, yet educational attainment remains modest with only 15.7 percent of residents holding a bachelor’s degree or higher, suggesting the wage premium is likely tied to specific industrial sectors—historically mining and related trades—rather than a broad knowledge economy. The median age of 36.1 years indicates a workforce in its prime earning years, supporting the observed income growth. Over the past decade, the RPP has remained remarkably stable, inching up only 0.2 percent from 100.558 in 2010 to 100.742 in 2023, while nominal median income surged 45.7 percent from $51,628 and median rent rose a comparatively modest 37.3 percent from $415. Wages have decisively outpaced both inflation and shelter costs, expanding real disposable income considerably over the period. For a prospective resident, the budgeting calculus centers on the trade-off between ultra-low housing outlays and persistently high service expenditures. A household budget benefits immediately from rental costs requiring less than one-tenth of gross income, a rarity that allows for aggressive savings or absorption of the 8.6 percent service premium. However, the low educational attainment figure and sector-specific employment base imply income volatility risk; a disruption in the primary local industry would remove the wage advantage that currently makes the service premium manageable. New arrivals should price healthcare plans, utility deposits, and vehicle maintenance—costs that track the 108.6 service index—before assuming the headline rent figure defines the total cost of living.

What this means for residents of Greenlee County, Arizona

With a Purchasing Power Index of 74684.8, a median household income of $75,239 in Greenlee County, Arizona has the equivalent buying power of $56,192,097 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 83.3 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
