What this means for residents of Missaukee County, Michigan

With a Purchasing Power Index of 65610.4, a median household income of $61,868 in Missaukee County, Michigan has the equivalent buying power of $40,591,842 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 99.7 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
