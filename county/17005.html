📊 Local Market Analysis

Bond County’s cost of living sits just below the national average, with the BEA Regional Price Parities index at 98.8 versus the benchmark 100. Goods prices are modestly higher than the countrywide level at 101.6, while services are noticeably cheaper at 92.4. Housing, measured by rental costs, is the most affordable component, registering an index of 80.4—nearly 20 % below the national norm. The median monthly rent of $735 further underscores the housing advantage, falling $193 short of the national median rent of $928. The structure of local expenses is driven primarily by the goods sector. At 101.6, the goods index exceeds both services and housing, indicating that everyday items such as groceries, apparel, and fuel cost slightly more than the average American. However, the lower service index (92.4) and especially the deep housing discount (80.4) offset the higher goods prices, producing a net environment that feels inexpensive for most households. Daily life is therefore characterized by affordable shelter and utilities, while the modest premium on consumables is the only area where residents pay a touch more. Disposable income calculations reveal a comfortable margin after rent. With a median household income of $61,603, the median renter spends $735 per month on housing, representing 14.3 % of income—well under the conventional 30 % rent‑burden threshold. Subtracting rent leaves roughly $55,000 of annual earnings for other expenses, taxes, and savings. The Purchasing Power Index of 62,381.5 quantifies this advantage: residents can purchase about 62 % of the goods and services that a typical American household can buy with the same nominal income, reflecting the combined effect of lower housing costs and cheaper services. In real terms, the median worker retains more purchasing power than the national average despite earning $3,444 less than the national median income. Local demographics shape this cost profile. Only 12.3 % of the population holds a bachelor’s degree or higher, and the median age of 40.4 suggests a mature workforce with limited inflow of high‑earning, highly educated professionals. The unemployment rate of 4.3 % aligns closely with the national rate, indicating a stable labor market but not a particularly dynamic one. The modest educational attainment and steady employment levels help keep wage pressures low, which in turn sustains the relatively inexpensive service and housing sectors. Over the past decade, the RPP fell from 101.78 in 2010 to 98.75 in 2023, a 3 % decline, while median household income rose from $47,946 to $61,603, a 28.5 % increase. Rent climbed from $608 to $735, a 20.9 % rise, yet remained far below the national average. Income growth outpaced both the modest decline in overall price levels and the increase in rent, indicating that wages have kept pace with, and indeed surpassed, local cost pressures. Prospective residents should budget for a slightly higher grocery and fuel bill than the national average, but can expect substantial savings on rent and utilities. Allocating roughly 14 % of income to housing leaves ample room for discretionary spending, transportation, and healthcare. A realistic monthly budget might include $300 for groceries, $150 for fuel and auto expenses, $120 for utilities and internet, and $200 for dining and personal services, leaving a sizable surplus for savings or debt repayment. Understanding this composition helps newcomers plan a household budget that leverages Bond County’s low housing costs while accommodating the modest premium on goods.

What this means for residents of Bond County, Illinois

With a Purchasing Power Index of 62381.5, a median household income of $61,603 in Bond County, Illinois has the equivalent buying power of $38,428,875 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 80.4 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
