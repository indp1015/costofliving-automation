📊 Local Market Analysis

Neosho County’s cost of living profile reveals a price environment that sits below the national benchmark but with notable internal stratification. The Bureau of Economic Analysis regional price parity index registers 89.9 for overall consumer costs, indicating that a dollar here purchases roughly 10 percent more than the same dollar would nationally. However, this aggregate masks significant variation across expenditure categories: goods, encompassing groceries, apparel, and fuel, register at 96.5, nearly aligning with national averages, while services—including healthcare, utilities, and dining out—come in at 68.6, reflecting a substantial discount. Rental costs, measured separately at 90.8, further confirm that housing expenses remain modest relative to the rest of the country, though they sit slightly above the services tier. For households navigating daily budgets, this means that routine purchases such as food and fuel will feel familiar in price movement, while discretionary and essential services represent a meaningful cost advantage, effectively lowering the barrier to maintaining a baseline standard of living. The consumption breakdown suggests that Neosho County’s affordability is anchored more by suppressed service prices than by rock-bottom good prices. With goods hovering just under the national 100 threshold, residents will not encounter the deep discounts on everyday commodities that might be found in lower-RPP regions, but the 68.6 services index implies that medical outlays, utility bills, and restaurant meals are considerably cheaper than the national norm. This structure renders daily life inexpensive not because core consumables are dirt cheap, but because the broader service basket—often the fastest-growing component of household spend—is deeply discounted. The rental cost component at 90.8 reinforces this picture: a median monthly rent of $650 consumes only 14.0 percent of the median household income of $55,70

What this means for residents of Neosho County, Kansas

With a Purchasing Power Index of 61956.7, a median household income of $55,704 in Neosho County, Kansas has the equivalent buying power of $34,512,360 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 90.8 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
