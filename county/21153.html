📊 Local Market Analysis

Magoffin County presents a cost of living profile defined by extreme divergence between goods and services, registering an overall Regional Price Parity of 89.9 against the national baseline of 100. While the aggregate figure suggests a discount, the component indices reveal a distorted consumer landscape: Goods—encompassing groceries, apparel, and fuel—sit at 94.5, only marginally below the national average, whereas Services, including healthcare, utilities, and dining, plummet to 62.9, the lowest cost driver in the local basket. Rental costs occupy a middle ground at 80.9. This structure indicates that daily essentials like food and transportation absorb a disproportionate share of household budgets relative to the national norm, while the steep discount on services likely reflects limited local supply—fewer providers, reduced competition, and constrained access—rather than genuine affordability for the consumer. The consumption breakdown confirms that Goods drive the local cost structure, creating a scenario where daily life is expensive for necessities but artificially cheap for discretionary or care-based spending. A median household income of $33,632—roughly 48 percent of the national median of $65,047—collides with this reality. Although the median monthly rent of $633 consumes a manageable 22.6 percent of gross income, significantly below the national rent burden implied by the $928 median, the Purchasing Power Index of 37,414.6 illustrates the limited reach of local wages. Adjusted for the 89.9 RPP, the real value of earnings barely exceeds the nominal figure, leaving minimal discretionary capacity after covering goods priced near national levels. The labor market context sharpens this pressure: a 12.4 percent unemployment rate and an educational attainment rate of just 4.5 percent for bachelor’s degrees or higher suggest a workforce concentrated in low-wage sectors with limited mobility, while a median age of 41.9 years indicates an established population with fixed healthcare and utility needs that the cheap services index may not adequately serve. Over the past decade, the RPP has remained remarkably stable, slipping only 0.6 percent from 90.433 in 2010 to 89.89 in 2023, signaling that the relative price structure is entrenched. However, nominal dynamics have shifted against residents. Median income rose 18.0 percent, from $28,500 to $33,632, yet median rent accelerated 32.4 percent, climbing from $478 to $633. This divergence means housing costs have grown nearly twice as fast as earnings, eroding the shelter affordability advantage that once existed. Because the RPP is flat, the rent surge likely reflects local supply constraints or income segmentation rather than broad inflation, but the result is a tightening budget squeeze for renters specifically. For a prospective resident, the budgeting calculus centers on the high floor for goods and the opacity of the services discount. Grocery and fuel bills will track close to national averages, demanding careful allocation on a sub-$35,000 income. The low services index (62.9) cannot be treated as savings; it often signals access gaps—longer drives for specialists, limited childcare options, or unreliable broadband—that impose hidden time and travel costs. Rent at $633 appears low, but the 32 percent decade-long increase warns that entry-level housing is tightening. A household budget here requires prioritizing goods inflation hedges and building contingency reserves for service access failures, as the nominal wage floor offers little buffer against either.

What this means for residents of Magoffin County, Kentucky

With a Purchasing Power Index of 37414.6, a median household income of $33,632 in Magoffin County, Kentucky has the equivalent buying power of $12,583,278 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 80.9 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
