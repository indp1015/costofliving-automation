What this means for residents of Winnebago County, Wisconsin

With a Purchasing Power Index of 78173.1, a median household income of $72,873 in Winnebago County, Wisconsin has the equivalent buying power of $56,967,083 in an average U.S. county.

Because the Housing Rent index is 89.5 (vs. the national average of 100), housing is the primary driver of the local cost of living. Prospective residents should budget carefully for rent or mortgage payments, even if everyday goods and services feel relatively affordable.
